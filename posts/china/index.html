Hong Kong, China

Hong Kong, China January, 2020 Ladies Market & Victoria Peak A whirlwind pass through Hong Kong on our way home. Time for dinner, the Ladies Market and a trip up to Victoria Peak. Luxurious rooms…by the hour. Evan is never without his water! Very looonnnng subway cars. A mix of old, new and nature at St. John’s Church in the middle of Hong Kong. A little bit of Hong Kong. I’ve never seen such density! Coming down the funicular from the Peak. It’s at such a steep pitch that the buildings appear to be built on an angle. Playtime in the city.
