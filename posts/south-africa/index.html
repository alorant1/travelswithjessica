Cape Town, South Africa

Cape Town, South Africa skip render: ucaddon_blox_line_text_divider Captivating Cape Town Cape Town in late December sits at the tip of Africa where two oceans make up their minds about each other. We were there with the kids for just over a week, working our way along the Cape Peninsula — and we could have easily stayed twice as long.  skip render: ucaddon_card_carousel The Cape of Good Hope is technically not the southernmost point of Africa — that’s Cape Agulhas, further east — but it is certainly the most dramatic. The cliffs are enormous and the wind is something else entirely. skip render: ucaddon_card_carousel Boulders Beach near Simon’s Town has an African penguin colony — which somehow still surprises every time. Penguins! In Africa! They waddle around completely unbothered by the visitors. Muizenberg, on False Bay, is known for its colourful wooden bathing boxes and its waves, which are just big enough to be fun. skip render: ucaddon_card_carousel The baboons at Cape Point are… bold. This one joined us for lunch uninvited.
