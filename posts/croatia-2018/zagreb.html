Zagreb

Finishing our Croatian adventure on day 10 in the very cool city of Zagreb. This place definitely needs some more time to explore…next time.

Day 10!

Fantastic wine tasting and education at Vinoteka Borenstein. I definitely recommend a visit to see owners Ivan and Doris next time you’re here.

My new friend...not much of a conversationalist

Lotrscak tower. Noon canon goes off daily, harking back to the times when we needed to synchronize our watches and sundials.

Street art commemorating famous inventors from Croatia. Here is Penkola who invented the pencil. A little further along is Nikola Tesla.

Lots of old gaslights around the parliament. There is a guy who actually walks around and hand-lights each one. This involves flicking a switch in each fixture with a long stick.

St Mark’s Church.
