Zadar, Croatia

Zadar, Croatia May, 2018 Krka National Park Loving the town of Zadar where we have landed for the night after a walk through Krka National Park. The park has a buildup of travertine (limestone deposits from the ground water) that has formed into pools and has built stunning waterfalls. An hour away is the small town of Zadar where the boardwalk has the most amazing sea symphony, pipes amplifying sounds from the sea that resemble whale song. Our new chariot. The whole run of the waterfalls. Roman ruins of an amphitheater were uncovered when the rubble was cleared after relentless days of bombing during WWII. The six wells in the town square serves the people of Zadar into the mid 19th century. Day 8! Fabulous dinner this evening. The Croatian wine industry is pretty exceptional. Meena listening to the whale song. Plank over the symphony holes. Post dinner symphony. Previous Next Plitvice Lakes National Park Gorgeous day hiking at Plitvice Lakes National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Sixteen large and small lakes connected by tons of waterfalls.
